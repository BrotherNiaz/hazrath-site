    Toyota Supra

    The A80, also known as the Mk4

    Engine
    3.0 L 2JZ-GTE inline-six, sequential twin-turbo
    Power
    320 hp in the US, officially 276 hp in Japan
    Drive
    Rear-wheel drive, 6-speed manual
    Built
    1993 to 2002

    Hazrath’s note: Ask him how much power a stock-block 2JZ can handle. Whatever number you guess, his is higher.

    The S2000’s original engine made about 120 horsepower per litre without a turbo, one of the highest outputs for a naturally aspirated production car of its time. The Supra came back in 2019 as the GR Supra, co-developed with BMW. Hazrath has thoughts about that. Several hours of thoughts.
